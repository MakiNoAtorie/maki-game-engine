# The run by hand of stage 65, by the pad only (no key of the game): an input script of
# the editor (crates/devtools/src/input_script.rs). The room is room_d: the player starts
# at (0, 0, 0); the sign at (2.6, 0, 0) asks a question: "Open it" opens the door in the
# back wall. The pause menu has no pad button: `P` opens it, the pad moves and closes it.
# A config for it: [rooms] start = "game/assets/rooms/room_d.ron", start_spawn = 3,
# [window] focus = false, [audio] output = false, [save] enabled = false; and once more
# with [walk] mode = "Grid": the stick moves cell by cell.
# maki-editor game CONFIG --input-script tools/editor_scripts/stage65.txt --input-shots DIR
wait 90
tap F5
wait 60
shot play

# Half a tilt to the right: half the walk speed; then a full tilt for as long.
stick left 0.5 0
wait 40
stick left 0 0
wait 10
shot half
stick left 1 0
wait 40
stick left 0 0
wait 10
shot full

# Run (Pad X) up to the sign.
press Pad X
stick left 1 0
wait 30
stick left 0 0
release Pad X
wait 10
shot run

# The question: D-pad down to "Open it", Pad A picks it.
tap Pad A
wait 30
shot ask
tap Pad down
wait 10
shot ask_down
tap Pad A
wait 90
shot door_open

# Again: Pad A takes "Leave it"; its message closes with Pad A.
tap Pad A
wait 30
tap Pad A
wait 30
shot left_message
tap Pad A
wait 20

# The pause menu: the D-pad moves it, Pad B closes it.
tap P
wait 30
shot pause
tap Pad down
wait 10
shot pause_down
tap Pad B
wait 30
shot unpaused

# A diagonal tilt: on the grid, one cell forward (the dominant axis; the cell at the
# left has the button). Half a tilt (above) is under the grid tilt: no grid move.
stick left -0.4 0.7
wait 30
stick left 0 0
wait 30
shot diagonal
tap F5
wait 30
quit
